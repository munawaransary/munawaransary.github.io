A free, open-source Bangla AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) app that gives non-verbal children a voice. Children tap picture tiles and the app speaks a full, grammatical Bangla sentence. It pairs a deterministic grammar engine (the safety-critical path) with a fine-tuned BanglaT5 neural composer trained on self-generated, order-shuffled data — reaching near-perfect exact match on tile combinations it never saw in training.
